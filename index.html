    INTERSTELLAR LENS

    Point your phone. Know the sky.

    Interstellar Lens is made for your phone. It uses your camera, compass and motion sensors to label the real sky — planets, stars, constellations, deep-sky objects and satellites, live.

    1. Open your phone's camera and scan the code.
    2. Tap Start sky view and allow camera, motion and location.
    3. Hold your phone up to the sky. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen to install.
